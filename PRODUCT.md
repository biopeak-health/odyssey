# BIOPEAK Event Capture

## Register

**product** — single-purpose capture form that serves the event workflow (collect lead → deliver brochure). Splash is brand moment; form is product UI matching Biopeak admin theme.

## Users & Purpose

- **Who:** CXO / healthcare leaders scanning a QR at a live Biopeak event.
- **Context:** Phone in hand, noisy venue, short attention, often on event Wi‑Fi.
- **Job:** Enter name + phone once, get the CXO brochure, leave a row in the event sheet.
- **Primary task:** Submit contact details; download PDF.

## Brand personality

Calm, clinical, trustworthy — light healthcare-tech product feel from Biopeak admin UI.

## Anti-references

- Dark luxury landing / Didot hero pages
- Purple SaaS gradients, glassmorphism, hero metric strips
- Admin dashboard chrome (sidebar, tables) on this page — colors/theme only

## Design principles

1. One job: capture + deliver brochure.
2. Theme locked to Biopeak product screenshot (light grey, white surface, coral CTA).
3. Dedup and disable re-submit; people tap twice.
4. Fast path after splash; no marketing clutter.
